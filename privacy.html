Privacy Policy

WordMist · Effective October 18, 2026

WordMist is a vocabulary app that teaches English words through widgets. We built it to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else. All words, meanings and example sentences are included in the app, so it does not need the internet to work.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: which words you learned, forgot or are reviewing, your daily lists, history and streaks.
  • Your settings, such as levels, words per day, widget interval and reminder time.

The app and its widgets share this data on your device only. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS at the time you choose. No server is involved.

Pronunciation

Words are read aloud with the voices built into iOS, on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Backup

Like other app data, your progress can be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we cannot access it.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether your subscription is active, and keeps that on your device.

Children

WordMist collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/BagliApps/wordmist/issues.
