Support

WordMist

Found a bug, a wrong meaning or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/BagliApps/wordmist/issues. Please include your device model and system version, and for a word, the word itself.

Common questions

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for WordMist and pick a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize › Lock Screen and add WordMist from the widget area. The home page has short videos.

Will I lose my progress when I update?
No. Progress is kept on your device across updates.

I bought Premium on another device.
Use "Restore Purchases" in the Premium screen with the same Apple ID.

Where do the words come from?
From open data: the CEFR-J Wordlist, Octanove C1/C2, the New Academic Word List, Wiktionary, Tatoeba, CMUdict and FrequencyWords, plus entries we wrote ourselves. The full list with licenses is in the app under Settings › Sources and licenses.
